    Questions

    Can't find your answer? Send us a message.

    What exactly will I receive?

    One edition of Insight Out a month, by email. It's a curated set of news items on emerging trends, competitors' moves, new operating models and technology and AI. Every item tells you what happened, why it matters for the industry, and links directly to its source.

    Which briefs can I choose from?

    Five ready briefs, each with a free sample:

    • CEE Expansion & M&A Tracker: deals, foreign investment and ownership changes in Central and Eastern Europe.
    • Corporate Strategy & Pivot Signal Tracker: new strategies, market exits, divestments and business model shifts.
    • Enterprise AI, Tech & Partnerships Monitor: tech and AI partnerships, deployments with measurable results, corporate venturing.
    • Workforce Restructuring & Efficiency Monitor: layoffs, delayering, AI-driven reorganisations and site consolidation.
    • The Future of Workforce & Skills Intelligence: reskilling, entry-level hiring and new ways of managing talent.

    Need a different topic? That is what the Bespoke brief is for.

    What sources do you use?

    Only reliable, carefully selected, publicly available sources: company announcements and reports, earnings calls, publicly released analyst commentary, regulators and public bodies, reputable news outlets and industry associations.

    We don't use paid or premium intelligence databases. What you're paying for is the selection, verification and insight. Every item links to its original source so you can check it yourself.

    What's the difference between Catalogue and Bespoke?

    A Catalogue brief covers a topic we already follow, and you can start right away. A Bespoke brief is designed around your own topic, competitors and questions, and we agree the scope with you before you pay.

    Can I share it with my team?

    Yes, within your organisation. Many clients send it to a team distribution list. Please don't republish it or share it outside your organisation. Company branded briefs are made for company-wide distribution under your own branding.

    How do I cancel?

    Anytime, from the link in your payment receipt or by emailing us. Your subscription stays active until the end of the month you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

    Is this investment advice?

    No. The brief gives you context on industry developments to inform your own strategic or investment thinking. Decisions remain yours.
