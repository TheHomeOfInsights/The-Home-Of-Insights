3. The service

We offer three kinds of Brief:

  • Catalogue: a Brief from our current catalogue, delivered to one email address of your choice (which may be a team distribution list).
  • Bespoke: a Brief designed for you on a topic of your choice, following a scope we agree with you before you pay.
  • Company branded: a Bespoke Brief presented in your organisation's branding and prepared for distribution within your organisation.

Every Brief is compiled exclusively from publicly available sources, such as company announcements and reports, earnings calls, regulators and public bodies, news outlets, industry associations and published research. We do not use paid or premium intelligence databases. Each item is summarised in our own words, explained in terms of why it matters for the industry, and linked to its original source.

A Brief is a curated selection of developments that we judge relevant. It is not an exhaustive record of all activity in a field, and it is not investment, legal, tax or other professional advice.
